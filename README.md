# Free EM 385-1-1 Activity Hazard Analysis (AHA) Samples

Complete Activity Hazard Analysis examples for USACE and NAVFAC construction
work, written to the current **EM 385-1-1 (15 March 2024)** manual and laid
out to match **ENG Form 6206 (SEP 2024)**.

**Browse and download: <https://em385library.com>**

Free Word (.docx) and PDF downloads. No email gate, no signup.

## What's covered

Ten definable features of work, each with its own complete AHA:

| DFOW | |
|---|---|
| Asbestos abatement | Pile driving |
| Asphalt paving | Rebar and formwork |
| Dewatering and cofferdam operations | Site clearing and grubbing |
| Forklift and material handling | Temporary electrical power |
| HVAC / mechanical equipment installation | Underground utility installation |

Each sample includes:

- Job-step table with hazards, controls, and **residual RACs**
- Risk Assessment Code (RAC) matrix per EM 385-1-1 (2024)
- Equipment to be used
- Training requirements
- Inspection requirements
- Competent / qualified person requirements
- Applicable EM 385-1-1 (2024) sections and OSHA standards
- Employee sign-off sheet

## Important

These are **samples to adapt, not documents to submit as-is**. An AHA has to
reflect your actual site, crew, sequence, and equipment. Every AHA still
requires review and acceptance by the SSHO and the government's designated
representative before work starts.

They were written from the manual. They are not a substitute for review by a
qualified person familiar with your project.

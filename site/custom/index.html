Frequently asked

Which edition do you cite?

EM 385-1-1, 15 March 2024 by default. If your contract was solicited before 15 March 2024 it stays on the 30 Nov 2014 edition — tell us in the activity field and we cite the 2014 numbering instead.

Is this a signed, ready-to-submit document?

No — no outside vendor can honestly sell you that. An AHA must be prepared for actual site conditions and signed by your competent person and reviewed by your SSHO (para 2-6). What we deliver is the complete, correctly formatted, correctly cited draft that gets you 90% of the way there.

Can we order several / get an invoice?

Yes — order each activity separately, or describe all activities in one order's field and we'll follow up with a combined quote. A receipt is issued automatically at checkout; reply to it for anything your accounting team needs.

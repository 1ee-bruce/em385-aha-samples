About this library

EM 385 AHA Library is run by Bruce, a technical documentation specialist. Every sample is drafted against the current edition of the manual — EM 385-1-1, 15 March 2024 — and its paragraph citations are checked against the manual text before publishing. Layouts follow ENG Form 6206 (SEP 2024). The downloads are free and un-gated because the fastest way to evaluate a resource is to open it. This site is not affiliated with USACE; every AHA must be adapted to actual site conditions and reviewed by your competent person / SSHO before submission.

Questions, corrections, or an activity you'd like added to the free library: support@em385library.com — we read everything.

Disclaimer: Sample AHAs on this site are drafting aids formatted to EM 385-1-1 (2024) conventions. Every AHA must be adapted to your site's actual conditions and reviewed by your competent person / SSHO before submission and use. This is not professional safety or legal advice.

Glossary: DFOW = definable feature of work · RAC = risk assessment code · SSHO = site safety and health officer · KO / COR = the government contracting officer / the officer's on-site representative ("the Corps reviewer") · CP/QP = competent / qualified person.
